Cloud providers like OpenAI Realtime and Gemini Live ship one speech-to-speech model. OpenToys gets the same experience locally by chaining three models on your Mac.

```
ESP32-S3 mic ──ws──▶ VAD ─▶ Whisper (STT) ─▶ LLM (MLX) ─▶ TTS ──ws──▶ ESP32-S3 speaker
```

## Connection flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Toy boots">
    The ESP32 starts an open access point called `ELATO` at `192.168.4.1`. The LED glows pink while it waits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mac joins and handshake">
    The toy probes ports `49320` and `8000` on your Mac until the OpenToys server answers, then opens a websocket. The LED turns white.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conversation">
    Mic audio streams up, synthesized speech streams back. Press **End** in the app to close the session and view the transcript.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **`ELATO` isn't listed:** the toy isn't powered or the firmware didn't flash. Reflash and watch for the pink LED.
- **Joined but nothing happens:** make sure OpenToys is running on the Mac.
- **Play is greyed out:** wait for the status row to read `Ready on device`.
