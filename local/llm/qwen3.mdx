---

Qwen3, Qwen3.5 and Qwen3.8 models (0.6B up to 27B in the README) from [`mlx-community`](https://huggingface.co/mlx-community) work with OpenToys and are a good fit if your toy speaks Chinese or another non-English language. Paste the repo name into the **Models** page to switch.

<Note>
  Qwen3 can produce reasoning text before its answer. OpenToys strips it before the reply goes to text-to-speech.
</Note>
