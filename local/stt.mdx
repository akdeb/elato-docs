OpenToys transcribes your toy's microphone audio with **Whisper large-v3-turbo**, loaded through [`mlx-audio`](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-audio).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Model** | `mlx-community/whisper-large-v3-turbo-asr-fp16` |
| **Runs on** | Apple Silicon via MLX |
| **Languages** | Multilingual (English, Chinese, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, German, Italian and more) |

## How it works

1. The ESP32-S3 captures audio from an I2S microphone (INMP441) and streams it to your Mac over a websocket.
2. Voice activity detection finds the end of your sentence.
3. Whisper transcribes the utterance and hands the text to the [LLM](/local/llm).

<Note>
  The model downloads on first setup into the app data directory. After that it works offline.
</Note>
