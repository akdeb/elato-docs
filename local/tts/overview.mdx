OpenToys ships ten TTS backends. Pick one on the **Models** page and swap it live. Qwen3-TTS is the default and the best fit for most Apple Silicon Macs.

| Backend | Best for | RAM | Page |

## Choosing a backend

Check the licenses before shipping a product. Voxtral, Higgs Audio and rumik-oss are non-commercial or research-only.

## Cloning a voice

Most backends clone a voice from less than 10 seconds of audio. Voxtral and rumik-oss use built-in voices instead.

Add a voice with a short, clean reference clip and its transcript, then assign it to a character. If a character's voice isn't on disk yet, the Play button shows **Download voice**.
