**OpenToys** is the local-first version of Elato. Your Apple Silicon Mac runs every model, and your ESP32-S3 streams audio to it over a websocket. Nothing leaves your home.

<Card title="OpenToys on GitHub" icon="github" href="https://github.com/akdeb/OpenToys" horizontal>
  Download the app, flash the firmware and start building.
</Card>

## The local stack

| Stage | Model | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **STT** (speech to text) | Whisper large-v3-turbo | [STT](/local/stt/whisper) |
| **LLM** (the brain) | Any `mlx-community` model, default Ministral-3-3B | [LLM](/local/llm/overview) |
| **TTS** (text to speech) | Qwen3-TTS and Chatterbox-turbo | [TTS](/local/tts/overview) |
| **Pipeline** | STT → LLM → TTS over a websocket | [Realtime](/local/realtime/pipeline) |

All models run through [MLX](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx), Apple's machine learning framework, so you need an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer).

## Cloud or local?

| | Cloud AI | Local AI |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Pay per API usage | Free |
| Privacy | Audio goes to the provider | Stays on your Mac |
| Latency | Native realtime models | Cascaded STT → LLM → TTS |
| Hardware | ESP32 + internet | ESP32 + Apple Silicon Mac |

Looking for OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, Eleven Labs or Hume? See [Cloud AI](/models/openai).

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install OpenToys">
    Download the latest DMG from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/akdeb/OpenToys/releases).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flash your ESP32-S3">
    In OpenToys, open **Settings** and pick **Connect your ESP32 Here**, then click **Flash**. Wiring is covered in the [firmware guide](/blog/firmware).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the toy's WiFi and press Play">
    The toy hosts an open network called `ELATO`. Join it from your Mac and press **Play**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  While your Mac is on `ELATO` it has no internet. Download models and voices before joining.
</Tip>
