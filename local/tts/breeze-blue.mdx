| **Model** | `mlx-community/Breeze-TTS-2-mlx-4bit` |
| **Memory** | About 8 GB RAM, 3 GB on disk |
| **Quality** | Expressive |
| **Voice cloning** | Yes |

Expressive voice design and cloning. It is heavier than Qwen3-TTS.

Characters can use paralinguistic cues in parentheses: `(laugh)`, `(chuckle)`, `(sigh)`, `(gasp)`, `(cough)`, `(clear throat)`, `(sniff)`, `(groan)` and `(shush)`.
