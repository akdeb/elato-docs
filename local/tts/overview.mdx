OpenToys ships two TTS backends. Both support **voice cloning from less than 10 seconds of audio**.

| Backend | Model |
|---|---|
| **Qwen3-TTS** (default) | `mlx-community/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-Base-4bit` |
| **Chatterbox-turbo** | `mlx-community/chatterbox-turbo-fp16` |

## Choosing a backend

Switch between them in the **Models** page. The backend can be swapped live.

## Cloning a voice

Add a voice with a short, clean reference clip and its transcript, then assign it to a character. If a character's voice isn't on disk yet, the Play button shows **Download voice**.

<Info>
  Want cloud voice cloning instead? See [Eleven Labs](/models/eleven-labs) and [Hume](/models/hume).
</Info>
