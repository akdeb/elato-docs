---

Gemma 4 models (26B A4B and 12B in the README) from [`mlx-community`](https://huggingface.co/mlx-community) work with OpenToys. Open the **Models** page, paste the Hugging Face repo name of the Gemma build you want and OpenToys loads it with `mlx-lm` (or `mlx-vlm` if the model needs it).

<Tip>
  Pick a 4-bit build to keep responses fast.
</Tip>
