A 35B mixture-of-experts model with multiple speakers. It is the heaviest backend here.

<Warning>
  Needs a Mac with 32 GB or more of memory and `mlx-audio` newer than 0.5.9.
</Warning>

Characters can use the cues `[laughs]`, `[sighs]` and `[whispers]`.
